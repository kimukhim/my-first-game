How to play

🚶 Walk with the arrow keys or WASD. Press Space to use your tool on the square in front of you (it has a white outline). Tap an arrow quickly to turn without walking.
🖱️ Or just click any square with the mouse: the farmer walks there and uses your tool on it.
🧰 Switch tools with 1 2 3 4, and change seeds with Q.
⛏️ Dig the light green field → 🌱 plant seeds → 💧 water them every day → ✋ harvest when they're ready.
📦 Put your harvest in the shipping bin next to your house. You get paid the next morning.
🏪 Buy new seeds at the shop. 💧 Refill your watering can at the pond.
🐔 Pet your chicken every day and she'll lay an egg for you.
🏠 Go into your house to sleep. Plants only grow overnight if they were watered! Don't stay up past midnight or you'll faint. 😵
