Controls

  • P1  ↑←↓→ move · Space drop balloon
  • P2  TFGH move · R drop balloon (2-player games)
  • P or Esc pauses.
  • Get splashed and you're trapped in a bubble. It turns darker blue for 4 seconds, then bursts. Only a needle gets you out. A rival who touches your bubble pops you out of the round. In co-op, a partner can save you.
  • Every game has a 3-minute clock.
  • Characters: Miko (monkey suit), Kuma (bear suit), Kero (frog suit) and Hop (bunny suit)

Power-ups hidden in crates

  • Extra balloon: one more balloon on the board at a time
  • Water drop: splashes reach one tile farther
  • Sneakers: run faster
  • Shield: a spinning gold ring that blocks one hit
  • Turtle ride: slow but steady
  • Kangaroo ride: super fast. Press your drop key facing a balloon to punch it 3 tiles, or punch enemies
  • Duck ride: fast, and flies over crates and balloons
  • Every ride takes one splash for you, then runs off.
  • Golden drop: splash range jumps to max
  • Poison: a random curse for 10 seconds (slow feet, reversed controls, balloon hiccups or tiny splash). Touch another player to pass it on!
  • Needle: pops you out of a bubble. Press your drop key while trapped (hold up to 3)
