How to play

1. Pick a seed at the top and click an empty soil square to plant it.
2. Pick the 💧 watering can and click your plants. Watered plants grow twice as fast.
3. When a plant is fully grown and bouncing, click it to harvest and earn coins.
4. Use your coins to buy better seeds and more land.
🏆 Goal: earn 1000 coins to become a Master Farmer!
